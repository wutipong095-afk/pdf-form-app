TH Sarabun New / TH Sarabun (THSarabun*.ttf)

TH Sarabun New is one of the thirteen Thai national fonts (ฟอนต์แห่งชาติ)
commissioned in Thailand and released for free public use, including
commercial use, modification, and embedding in documents and software.

Original author: Suppakit Chalermlarp.
Released through the national font programme (SIPA / Department of
Intellectual Property, Thailand).

The application distributes this font under those free-use terms. The
distributor has verified that commercial redistribution and embedding of
this font is permitted.
