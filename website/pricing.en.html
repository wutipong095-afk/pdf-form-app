How to get a license

  1. Download Setup and try the sample forms.
  2. Copy the 16-character machine ID from the license bar.
  3. Pay, then email the ID to fromdd@xambrain.com.
  4. Paste the key in the app. You do not reinstall.

Keys are not issued instantly on this site yet. Typical turnaround is one business day. You do not need the machine ID before you pay.

Policy

  • Up to 2 PC replacements per license per year when the old PC is retired
  • Works offline after activation. We do not host your PDFs
